# The Can Crusher

The project involves designing and building a pneumatic can crusher that uses air pressure to compress aluminum cans for recycling. The system uses two air resivoirs which lead to two actuating pistons to produce linear motion on both ends of the can that crushes cans from about 5 inches to 1 inch high. Key components include: base frame for structural stability, pneumatic pistons to generate the crushing force, crushing plates connected to two pushers each to crush the can, a catching bucket below the crusher for effecient catching and later disposal of crushed cans, and an Arduino Uno controlled electrical system which controls the solenoid valves to initiate movement, the button, a infared sensor to detect human movement within the build in order to act as a safety to protect from harm. Operation is controlled by both a button and  a manual pneumatic valve, which directs compressed air to extend the piston for crushing, then reverses airflow to retract it, readying the crusher for the next can. However, as the ending of the project, the crusher has shown to be inconsistent with its capability to crush, mostly due to the output force of the pushers not being sufficient enough to consistently crush the can
